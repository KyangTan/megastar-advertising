Our Vision and Mission

Creative and dynamic solutions for businesses. We create inspired, informative, and entertaining content that helps our clients' businesses succeed.

We are committed to providing customers with a reliable and responsible one-stop service. Giving the best quality products to customers.

Our Story

Megastar Advertising Signcraft was established in 1999 with Advertising and Large Format Printing as its core business. We are a signboard manufacturer company with more than 20 years of experience. We've evolved from a humble signage manufacturer to a comprehensive one-stop center by providing signage fabrication and installation to meet our clients' objectives.

We are specializing in a wide range of products, including 3D signage, project signboard, billboard, truck sticker, lightbox, acrylic signage, LED signage, and more.
